## Instructions

Create **one standalone 2D cartoon illustration** depicting the specified scene. The result will be used as an individual row illustration in a visual daily-routine pictogram chart.

### 1. Character & Clothing Consistency
- Use the character from Image 1 as the main subject.
- Keep the character recognizable and visually consistent across every generated image.
- Preserve the clothing, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth style, hairstyle, skin tone, and overall proportions directly from Image 1.
- Adapt the character’s pose, facial expression, and hand positions naturally to the requested activity.
- Make the character friendly, cheerful, approachable, and easy to understand at a glance.

### 2. Illustration Style
- Clean, polished 2D cartoon illustration.
- Bold, thick, smooth black outlines around the character and objects.
- Flat, vibrant colors with simple, subtle cel shading.
- Rounded shapes, clear silhouettes, and expressive facial features.
- Use the same colorful, friendly educational pictogram style as Image 2.
- Keep details simple and readable, even at small sizes.
- Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, sketchy lines, complex textures, and excessive details.

### 3. Scene Composition
- Create only the illustration for the requested scene, not an entire pictogram row or chart.
- Show the character performing the specified activity clearly and naturally.
- Include the relevant props, furniture, equipment, and environmental details mentioned in the scene description.
- Arrange the character and objects in a balanced, compact, horizontal composition.
- Use a natural three-quarter or front-facing view where appropriate.
- Include only enough background elements to establish the setting, such as a washing machine, bedside table, window, showerhead, or dining table.
- Keep the character as the main focal point.
- Make sure important objects are not cropped, hidden, or obscured.
- Use a consistent visual scale and level of detail across all scenes.

### 4. Transparent Background — Critical
- The entire background must be genuinely transparent, including all areas behind and around the character and objects.
- Output a PNG with a real alpha channel.
- Do not include a white background, colored background, blue wall, gradient, backdrop, floor plane, or background rectangle.
- Do not include an outer border, rounded rectangular frame, card, panel, or enclosing outline around the illustration.
- Do not render a checkerboard pattern; transparency must be actual transparency, not a visual representation of it.
- Keep all foreground elements fully colored and intact, with clean, smooth outlines and natural antialiasing.
- Background elements that are part of the scene, such as a washing machine or bedside table, should remain visible. Only the surrounding background should be transparent.

### 5. Output Requirements
- One image only, depicting one scene.
- Wide, horizontal composition suitable for placement inside a pictogram row.
- Center the complete illustration and use the available canvas efficiently without cutting off any part of the character or props.
- No text, letters, captions, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, or symbols unrelated to the scene.
- No extra characters unless specifically requested.
- Do not include the original reference images or combine multiple activities into one image.

**Final priority:** Character consistency, clear activity recognition, clean cartoon outlines, and genuine transparent PNG output. The finished illustration must be ready to place directly onto a colored background without needing to remove a background or frame afterward.
