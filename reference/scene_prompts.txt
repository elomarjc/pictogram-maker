# MASTER PROMPT SCENE LISTE (K-PIKTOGRAMMER)

1. MORGEN, BAD & PERSONLIG HYGIEJNE
================================================================================
Morgenrutine / Vågne op:
The character is stretching his arms happily next to his bed in the morning, with a fluffy pillow and neat duvet behind him.

Rede seng:
The character is making his bed neatly, smoothing out a clean duvet and plumping a soft white pillow.

Badetid / Brusebad:
The character is taking a shower, happily washing his hair with shampoo suds, with clean water drops spraying from the showerhead.

Børste tænder:
The character is standing at the bathroom sink brushing his teeth with a toothbrush, with toothpaste foam and a small cup on the shelf.

Tage tøj på:
The character is putting on his clean clothes and zipping up his grey hoodie with a cheerful smile.

================================================================================
Medicin (Morgen / Aften):

Drikke et glas vand:
The character is drinking a fresh glass of water, holding the clear glass up with a satisfied smile.

3. MAD & DRIKKE (MÅLTIDER)
================================================================================
The & Kaffe:
The character is sitting at a cozy table happily holding a warm steaming coffee mug, with a classic coffee pot on the table.

Morgenmad:
The character is sitting at the table eating breakfast, with a bowl of cereal, milk carton, and a spoon in hand.

Frokost (Rugbrødsmadder):
The character is sitting at the dining table eating open-faced sandwiches from a plate, with a knife and fork beside it.

Aftensmad:
The character is eating dinner, holding a fork and knife, with a warm plate of food and a glass on the dining table.

Bolle / Eftermiddagshygge:
The character is sitting at the table enjoying a freshly baked bread roll with butter, holding it happily with both hands.

Snack / Frugt:
The character is sitting at a table eating a fresh red apple and a peeled banana from a fruit bowl.

4. PLIGTER & HJEMLIGE OPGAVER
================================================================================
Hænge vasketøj op:
The character is hanging clean laundry up on a drying rack with clothespins, with a laundry basket standing next to him.

Lægge tøj sammen:
The character is standing at a table neatly folding clean colorful t-shirts and placing them in a tidy stack.

Vaske op i hånden:
The character is washing dishes at the sink, with gentle soap bubbles, a dish brush, clean plates on a rack, and a soap bottle.

Tømme / Fylde opvaskemaskine:
The character is placing a clean plate into the open rack of a dishwasher, smiling as the kitchen stays tidy.

Gå med skrald & flasker (Skraldespand / Pant):
The character is carrying a tied green trash bag and a crate with empty soda bottles out to the recycling bin.

Støvsuge / Rengøring:
The character is vacuuming the floor with a modern vacuum cleaner, moving the nozzle smoothly across the floor.

Tørre bord af:
The character is wiping down a table with a damp cleaning cloth, leaving the surface clean and shiny.

Rydde op på værelset:
The character is putting books and hobby items into a storage box, keeping his room organized and neat.

================================================================================

Lægge puslespil / Brætspil:
The character is sitting at a table thoughtfully and happily fitting pieces into a colorful jigsaw puzzle.

Høre musik / Hovedtelefoner:
The character is wearing comfortable headphones around his head, tapping his foot and happily listening to his favorite music.

Gåtur / Frisk luft:
The character is walking happily outside along a park path, swinging his arms lightly with green trees in the background.

Cykeltur:
The character is riding a bicycle happily wearing a safety helmet, with hands firmly on the handlebars.

Spille bold / Fodbold:
The character is kicking a soccer ball on a green lawn with a joyful and energetic expression.

Svømmehal / Svømmetur:
The character is at the swimming pool wearing swim shorts and a towel over his shoulder, smiling near the clean blue pool water.

Læse i bog eller blad:
